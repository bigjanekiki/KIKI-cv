Independent Project独立项目

Healthy Savings · 健康储蓄罐

独立构思与开发 | Independently Conceptualised and Developed

中文介绍

一个将健康饮食行为与虚拟储蓄机制结合的个人行为管理工具。

English Description

A personal behaviour management tool that combines healthy eating habits with a virtual savings mechanism.

The website features behavioural rewards and spending records, a separate food-awareness journal, monthly reviews, fasting tracking, and data backup.

核心功能 | Key Features

  • 健康行为奖励与饮食行为支出记录
    Healthy behaviour rewards and food-related spending records
  • 饮食觉察日记，独立于健康银行流水
    A food-awareness journal, separate from the health bank transaction records
  • 月度饮食行为回顾
    Monthly reviews of eating behaviours
  • 健康银行流水与累计储蓄统计
    Health bank transaction history and accumulated savings statistics
  • 断食计时与历史记录
    Fasting timer and history records
  • 数据备份与导出
    Data backup and export
